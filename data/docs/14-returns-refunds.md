# Returns and Refunds

## Return window
You can return a fan within 30 days of the delivery date. Seasonal promotions may extend this window; see the current holiday returns notice.

## Condition and fees
- Unopened in original packaging: full refund
- Opened but not installed, with all parts: refund minus a 10 percent restocking fee
- Installed, or with mounting damage: not returnable. Use the warranty for faults instead.

## Return shipping
Return shipping is free if the fan arrived damaged or defective. Otherwise a 15 US dollar return shipping fee is deducted from the refund.

## Refund timing
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned fan is inspected at the warehouse.

## Starting a return or exchange
Ask the assistant to open a support ticket with your order ID. You can exchange a fan for a different size once within the 30-day window; the price difference is charged or refunded.
