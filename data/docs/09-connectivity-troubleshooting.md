# Troubleshooting Connectivity

## Fan shows offline in the app
Check that the router is on and other devices are online. Switch the fan off at the breaker for 30 seconds. If the fan stays offline and the canopy light blinks red four times, see error E4.

## Setup fails at "Connecting to Wi-Fi"
The most common cause is a 5 GHz-only network. Many routers broadcast 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz under one name (band steering); if setup fails, enable a separate 2.4 GHz network name in your router settings or temporarily turn off 5 GHz. Double-check the Wi-Fi password, which is case-sensitive.

## Mesh networks
Mesh systems work if they broadcast 2.4 GHz. Place a mesh node within about 15 metres of the fan.

## After changing the router or password
The fan remembers only one network. Put it back into pairing mode and add it again in the app.

## Bluetooth "No device found"
Make sure the fan is in pairing mode, your phone is within 5 metres, and on Android that Bluetooth, Nearby Devices and location are enabled. If four phones are already paired, remove one or clear all pairings from the remote.
