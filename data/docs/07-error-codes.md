# Error Codes

## Reading error codes
When the fan detects a fault, the status light on the canopy blinks red in a repeating pattern and the Corvane Home app shows the code. The number of blinks equals the code number (E3 blinks three times, pauses, repeats).

## E1 to E3: motor faults
E1 Motor overload or stall: something is blocking the blades or the fan is badly unbalanced. Switch off, clear the obstruction and check blade screws. E2 Overheat: thermal protection has stopped the motor. The fan resets automatically after a 15-minute cool-down. Never attempt to bypass thermal protection. E3 Motor sensor fault (DC models only): a Hall-effect sensor inside the motor is not responding. Power cycle at the breaker for 30 seconds. If E3 returns, the motor needs service; for warranty purposes the sensor is part of the motor.

## E4 to E6: wireless faults
E4 Wi-Fi module not responding: switch off at the breaker for 30 seconds. If E4 returns, the receiver module needs replacing; the receiver counts as electronics under the warranty. E5 Bluetooth provisioning timeout: the 3-minute pairing window expired. Enter pairing mode again. E6 No remote paired or radio interference: re-pair the remote (see Remote Control).

## E7 to E9: system faults
E7 Firmware update failed: the fan automatically rolls back to the previous firmware. Retry the update when the fan has a stable Wi-Fi connection. E8 Light driver fault: the LED light kit's driver has failed; the fan itself keeps working. The light kit is an accessory under the warranty. E9 Supply voltage out of range: the fan needs 110 to 130 V. This is usually caused by a dimmer or variable-speed wall control. Replace it with a standard on/off switch, and contact an electrician if E9 continues.
