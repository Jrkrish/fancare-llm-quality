# Limited Warranty Policy (2022)

## Status of this policy
This policy was replaced by the 2025 warranty policy for purchases made on or after 1 January 2025. It still applies to fans purchased between 1 March 2022 and 31 December 2024.

## Coverage periods
- Motor: 3 years from purchase
- Electronics (receiver module, wireless module): 1 year from purchase
- Accessories (remote control, light kit, blades): 1 year from purchase

## Registration
Registration is recommended but does not extend any coverage period under this policy.

## Commercial use
Fans used in commercial premises are not covered under this policy.

## Exclusions and claims
Exclusions and the claim process are the same as the current policy: incorrect installation, outdoor use of indoor models, power surges and dimmer switches are excluded, and a serial number and proof of purchase are required.
