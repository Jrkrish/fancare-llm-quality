# Installation Guide

## Before you start
Switch off power at the circuit breaker, not just the wall switch. Installation takes about 90 minutes and needs two people for fans 56 inches and larger. You need a Phillips screwdriver, wire strippers, a voltage tester and a stepladder. If you are not comfortable with household wiring, hire a licensed electrician.

## What is in the box
Motor assembly, blades and blade irons, mounting bracket, 6-inch downrod, canopy, receiver module, remote control with wall cradle, CR2032 battery, balancing kit, and hardware bag. Light models also include the LED light kit.

## Mounting
The fan must hang from an electrical box marked "acceptable for fan support" and rated for at least 35 lb (16 kg). Standard light-fixture boxes are not acceptable. Sloped ceilings up to 30 degrees are supported with the included ball-joint hanger. For slopes between 30 and 45 degrees you need the SA-45 sloped ceiling adapter, sold separately.

## Downrod length
- Ceiling under 8 ft: use flush mount (MN30 and SW44 only) or the 6-inch downrod with other models only if blades stay at least 7 ft above the floor
- 9 ft ceiling: 12-inch downrod
- 10 ft ceiling: 18-inch downrod
- 12 ft ceiling: 36-inch downrod

## Wiring
Connect black (supply) to black, white (neutral) to white, and green or bare copper (ground) to the green ground screw. On light models, connect the blue light wire to black if you have a single wall switch. The receiver module sits inside the canopy between the supply wires and the motor.

## Finishing
Attach blades with the supplied screws and tighten them firmly. Fit the light kit if your model has one. Restore power at the breaker and pair the remote (see Remote Control). If the fan wobbles, use the balancing kit (see Noise and Wobble).
