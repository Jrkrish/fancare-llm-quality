# Holiday Returns Notice 2026

## Extended return window
Fans delivered between 15 November 2026 and 31 December 2026 can be returned until 31 January 2027, even if that is more than 30 days after delivery.

## Restocking fee waived
For deliveries in this period, the 10 percent restocking fee for opened but not installed fans is waived.

## What does not change
Installed fans are still not returnable. The 15 US dollar return shipping fee for non-defective returns still applies. Refund timing is unchanged.
