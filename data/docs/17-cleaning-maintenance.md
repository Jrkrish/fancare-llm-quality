# Cleaning and Maintenance

## Cleaning the blades
Switch the fan off and wait for it to stop. Clean blades every 3 months with a dry or slightly damp microfibre cloth. Do not use solvents, abrasive cleaners or furniture polish, which can damage the finish and are not covered by the warranty.

## Annual check
Once a year, switch off at the breaker and check that blade screws, the downrod pin and the mounting bracket are tight.

## Seasonal direction
On DC models, use Reverse in winter so the fan turns clockwise at low speed and pushes warm air down from the ceiling. In summer, use the normal anticlockwise direction for a cooling breeze. Basic models cannot reverse.

## Outdoor models
Wipe TerraBreeze models with a damp cloth and mild soap. Do not use a pressure washer or hose on the motor housing.
