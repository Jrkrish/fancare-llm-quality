# Orders and Shipping

## Order IDs
Every order has an ID in the format ORD- followed by five digits, for example ORD-10001. It is in the order confirmation email. The assistant can look up an order's status from its ID.

## Order statuses
Processing: the order is being prepared, usually 1 to 2 business days. Shipped: the order has left the warehouse and has a carrier and tracking number. Out for delivery: the carrier expects to deliver today. Delivered: the carrier has confirmed delivery. Cancelled: the order was cancelled and any payment refunded.

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days and is free on orders over 150 US dollars. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Corvane ships to the United States and Canada only.

## Cancelling or changing an order
Orders can be cancelled, or the delivery address changed, only while the status is Processing. After that, use the returns process.
