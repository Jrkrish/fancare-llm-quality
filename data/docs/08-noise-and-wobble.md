# Troubleshooting Noise and Wobble

## Wobbling
Some movement at high speed is normal. Excessive wobble is usually caused by loose blade screws, a blade that is bent, or a mounting bracket that is not tight. Switch off, tighten all blade and bracket screws, then use the balancing kit: clip the weight halfway along one blade at a time, run the fan, and stick the weight where the wobble is smallest. Stop using the fan if it wobbles severely after balancing.

## Humming
A humming motor on Basic (AC) models is almost always caused by a dimmer switch or variable-speed wall control. Replace it with a standard on/off switch. DC models should not hum; if one does, check for error codes.

## Clicking or rattling
Clicking usually comes from a loose light glass, canopy cover or blade screw. Tighten the light glass by hand until snug and check the canopy screws.

## Normal noise levels
DC models are rated at 32 dB or less at speed 1. Hush models are rated at 28 dB or less at speed 1. Firmware 3.2 made speed 1 slightly quieter on all DC models.
