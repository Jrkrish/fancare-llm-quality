# Voice Assistants

## Supported assistants
Corvane smart fans work with Amazon Alexa and Google Home. Basic (BS) models are not supported because they have no Wi-Fi.

## Linking
In the Alexa or Google Home app, add the Corvane skill or action and sign in with your Corvane account. Fans added to Corvane Home appear automatically with the names you gave them.

## Example commands
"Turn on the bedroom fan", "Set the living room fan to speed 3", "Turn off the fan light", "Set the fan light to 40 percent". Reverse direction and Breeze mode can only be changed in the Corvane Home app.

## Requirements
Voice control needs the fan to be connected to Wi-Fi with internet access. Bluetooth-only fans cannot be controlled by voice.
