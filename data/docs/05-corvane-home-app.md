# Corvane Home App Features

## Requirements
The Corvane Home app needs iOS 16 or later or Android 10 or later. A free Corvane account is required for Wi-Fi control; Bluetooth-only control works without an account.

## Schedules and timers
Create up to 10 schedules per fan, each with a time, days of the week, speed and light level. The sleep timer turns the fan off after 1 to 8 hours.

## Breeze mode
Breeze mode varies the speed randomly to imitate a natural breeze. It is available on DC models with firmware 3.1 or later.

## Energy reporting
DC models show estimated power use per day and per month. Basic models cannot be added to the app.

## Sharing
Invite up to 6 household members from Settings, then Share Home. Shared members can control fans and schedules but cannot remove the fan or change its Wi-Fi.
