# Safety Instructions

## Electrical safety
Always switch off power at the circuit breaker before installing, cleaning or servicing the fan. Follow local electrical codes. If you are unsure about any wiring, use a licensed electrician. Do not connect the fan to a dimmer or variable-speed wall control; use a standard on/off switch.

## Mounting and clearances
Mount only to an electrical box rated for fan support of at least 35 lb (16 kg). Blades must be at least 7 ft (2.1 m) above the floor and at least 18 inches (46 cm) from walls and sloped ceiling surfaces.

## Wet and outdoor locations
Only TerraBreeze (TB) models are wet-rated and may be installed outdoors, including where rain can reach them. All other models are for dry indoor locations only and must not be installed in bathrooms with showers, on open patios or in saunas.

## Built-in protections
The fan has thermal overload protection (error E2) and a stall detector (error E1). Never bypass, bridge or disable these protections. Do not operate a fan that wobbles severely or makes grinding noises; switch it off and contact support.

## Children and pets
Keep the remote away from small children; the coin battery is dangerous if swallowed. Seek medical help immediately if a battery is swallowed.
