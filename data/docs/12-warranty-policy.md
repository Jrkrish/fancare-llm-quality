# Limited Warranty Policy (2025)

## Who this policy covers
This policy applies to Corvane fans purchased on or after 1 January 2025 from Corvane or an authorised retailer. Fans purchased between 1 March 2022 and 31 December 2024 remain covered by the 2022 warranty policy.

## Coverage periods
- Motor (including motor sensors): 5 years from purchase
- Electronics (receiver module, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth module, DC motor controller): 2 years from purchase
- Accessories (remote control, LED light kit, blades and finish): 1 year from purchase

## Registration bonus
Registering the fan in the Corvane Home app within 60 days of purchase extends electronics coverage by 1 year, to 3 years in total. Motor and accessory coverage are not extended.

## Commercial use
Fans used in commercial premises (shops, restaurants, offices) are covered for 1 year on all components.

## What is not covered
Damage from incorrect installation, use of indoor-rated models outdoors or in wet locations, power surges, use with dimmer or variable-speed wall controls, and normal wear of finishes. The warranty is void if the serial number label is removed or the receiver module has been opened.

## Making a claim
You need the serial number (on the label inside the motor canopy) and proof of purchase. The assistant can check coverage from the serial number and open a support ticket. Corvane ships replacement parts free of charge; installation labour is not covered.
