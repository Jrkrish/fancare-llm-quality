# Wi-Fi Setup

## Requirements
Corvane smart fans connect to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks only. 5 GHz networks are not supported. Supported security is WPA2-Personal, and WPA3-Personal from firmware 3.2 onwards. Enterprise networks (WPA2-Enterprise) and networks that need a browser sign-in page (captive portals, as in hotels or dormitories) are not supported. Basic (BS) models have no Wi-Fi.

## Entering pairing mode
Turn the fan off and on at the wall switch three times within 10 seconds. The fan beeps twice and the status light blinks quickly. Pairing mode stays open for 3 minutes.

## Adding the fan in the app
Open the Corvane Home app, tap Add Device and keep your phone within 5 metres of the fan. The app finds the fan over Bluetooth Low Energy, then sends your Wi-Fi name and password to it. The fan joins Wi-Fi in about 30 seconds and the status light turns solid for 5 seconds.

## Placement
Keep the router within about 15 metres of the fan with no more than two walls between them. Metal ceilings and foil-backed insulation can weaken the signal.

## Changing networks
A fan stores one Wi-Fi network. If you change your router or Wi-Fi password, put the fan back into pairing mode and add it again. Your schedules are kept.
