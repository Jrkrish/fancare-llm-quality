# Bluetooth (BLE) Pairing and Local Control

## What Bluetooth is used for
The fan uses Bluetooth Low Energy 5.0 for two things: first-time setup (passing your Wi-Fi details to the fan) and local control from a nearby phone when Wi-Fi or the internet is down. Voice assistants and remote access away from home need Wi-Fi.

## Range and limits
Bluetooth range is about 10 metres in open space. Up to 4 phones can be paired over Bluetooth at the same time. A fifth phone will be refused until one is removed.

## Pairing a phone over Bluetooth
Enter pairing mode (power cycle three times within 10 seconds), then choose Bluetooth Only in the Corvane Home app. Enter the 6-digit PIN printed on the label inside the motor canopy. The pairing window closes after 3 minutes; if it closes, the app shows error E5 and you must enter pairing mode again.

## Android permissions
On Android, the app needs Bluetooth and Nearby Devices permissions, and location services must be switched on for scanning. If the app says "No device found", check these first.

## Removing all Bluetooth pairings
Hold the Fan Off and Light buttons on the remote together for 10 seconds. The fan beeps three times and forgets all paired phones. Wi-Fi settings are not affected.
