# Remote Control

## Overview
Every fan ships with a 433 MHz radio remote and a wall cradle. Radio does not need line of sight; range is about 12 metres. The remote uses one CR2032 coin battery, which lasts about 12 months.

## Buttons
DC models: speeds 1 to 6, Reverse (switches airflow direction for winter), Light on/off, press-and-hold Light to dim, and 1h/4h/8h timer buttons. Basic models: Low, Medium, High, Fan Off and Light only. Reverse is not available on Basic models.

## Pairing the remote to the fan
Restore power at the wall or breaker. Within 60 seconds, hold Speed 1 and Speed 6 together for 5 seconds (on Basic models, hold Low and High). The light blinks twice when pairing succeeds. If 60 seconds pass, switch power off and on and try again.

## Replacing the battery
Slide the back cover down, replace the CR2032 with the positive side facing up, and refit the cover. The remote does not need to be re-paired after a battery change.

## Remote not working
Replace the battery first. If the light on the remote does not flash when you press a button, the remote is faulty. If it flashes but the fan does not react, re-pair the remote. Replacement remotes are covered as accessories under the warranty.
