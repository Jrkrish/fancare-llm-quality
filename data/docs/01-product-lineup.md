# Corvane Ceiling Fan Product Lineup

## Product families
Corvane makes six fan families. Mini (MN) is a compact 30-inch fan for small rooms. AeroLite (AL) is an entry smart fan with an integrated LED light. Hush (HU) is the quiet range built around a low-noise DC motor. SkyWhirl (SW) is the flagship smart fan with DC motor and LED light. TerraBreeze (TB) is wet-rated for covered and open outdoor spaces. Loft (LX) is a 72-inch fan for large open-plan rooms. Basic (BS) is a non-smart AC fan controlled only by the remote.

## Model codes
The model code is two letters followed by the blade span in inches, for example SW52 is a 52-inch SkyWhirl and TB60 is a 60-inch TerraBreeze. The model code is also the first part of the serial number printed on the label inside the motor canopy.

## Choosing a fan size
Match blade span to floor area:
- Up to 75 sq ft: 29 to 36 inch
- 76 to 144 sq ft: 42 to 44 inch
- 145 to 225 sq ft: 50 to 54 inch
- 226 to 400 sq ft: 56 to 60 inch
- Over 400 sq ft: 62 inch or larger, or two fans

## DC and AC motors
All families except Basic use a brushless DC motor with six speeds, reverse (winter) mode and energy reporting in the Corvane Home app. Basic models use an AC motor with three speeds and no reverse function. DC models use up to 70 percent less energy than an equivalent AC fan.

## Smart features
Every family except Basic includes a combined Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz) and Bluetooth Low Energy module, works with the Corvane Home app, and supports voice assistants. Basic models have no wireless module and cannot be added to the app.
